The dashboard was the ask. What it needed was a registry — fifteen internal services that had grown up separately, different owners, different auth, and no shared answer to what exists, and can I use it.

So the shell owns nothing. Each service declares itself and the console renders from that. Adding one is a manifest entry, not a pull request. When a service goes down it renders as unavailable instead of taking the console down with it.

Fourteen months. One developer. The most useful thing I built is the part you cannot see.
