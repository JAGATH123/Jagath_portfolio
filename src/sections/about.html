▪ WHAT HAPPENED

They asked for a dashboard. I built the system under it. Nobody noticed. That was the point.

▾ the rest is underneath

The dashboard was the ask. A registry was the requirement. Fifteen internal services, grown up separately, different owners, different auth, and no shared answer to what exists, and may I use it.

So the shell owns nothing. Each service declares itself; the console renders what it is told. Adding one is a manifest entry, not a pull request. When a service goes down it renders as unavailable, and the console does not go down with it.

Fourteen months. One developer. The most useful thing I built is the part you cannot see.
